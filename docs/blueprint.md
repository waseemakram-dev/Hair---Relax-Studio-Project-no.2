# **App Name**: SalonFlow

## Core Features:

- Receipt Generation: Generate a receipt with customer name, selected portion, services, products, discounts, and total amount before service begins.
- Portion/Worker Tracking: Automatically track which portion/worker is selected, customer count per worker, worker sales, and total salon sales.
- Product Management: Add new products (name, price, quantity) and automatically update stock levels upon usage; display low stock alerts.
- Service Management: Add/edit/delete services with default prices and discount options.
- Sales Reporting: Automatically generate daily and monthly sales reports, downloadable in Excel format, showing individual worker sales and total salon sales.
- Receipt Printing: Print receipts directly with salon name, date, services, and total price; connect directly to a printer

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft lavender (#E6E6FA) to evoke a sense of calm and sophistication.
- Background color: Light grey (#F5F5F5) for a clean, modern feel.
- Accent color: Pale pink (#FFD1DC) to highlight key elements.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' for a modern yet welcoming aesthetic.
- Use minimalist icons related to salon services.
- Clean and well-organized layout for easy navigation.
- Subtle transitions for interactive elements and data loading.
